FleetBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

In short: FleetBook keeps your business records (vehicles, trips, expenses, parties and payments) on your phone. You sign in with your Google account, and FleetBook backs up your records to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. We have no servers and never receive your records. FleetBook has no ads and no analytics.

This policy explains how the FleetBook app ("FleetBook", "we") by Saadi Studios handles information.

Information you enter

FleetBook stores what you type into it:

  • Vehicles: name, registration number, make and model, and notes.
  • Parties (customers, suppliers, drivers): name, phone number, type, opening balance and notes.
  • Trips: date, route, customer, freight, amounts received, odometer readings and notes.
  • Expenses and payments: date, category, amount, amount paid, party, payment method and notes.
  • Settings: how often to back up, the Wi-Fi-only choice and your currency symbol.

These records are kept in FleetBook's private storage on your phone. FleetBook does not read your phone's contacts, location, photos or files. The only names and phone numbers it holds are the ones you type in.

Google sign-in

To use FleetBook you sign in with your Google account. Google gives FleetBook your name, email address and profile picture. FleetBook keeps only your name and email address, on your phone. It uses them to show which account is signed in and to find that account's backups. FleetBook never sees your Google password.

Backups to your Google Drive

  • With your permission, FleetBook gets one limited kind of access to Google Drive, which Google describes as "See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive" (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata). It lets FleetBook use a hidden folder that only FleetBook can open. FleetBook cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.
  • A backup is a compressed copy of all the records listed above. It goes directly from your phone to your Google Drive over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We never receive it.
  • Backups run daily or weekly, as you choose (you can also turn them off), and whenever you tap Back up now. The newest 10 backups are kept, and older ones are deleted automatically.
  • When you sign in on a new phone, FleetBook lists the backups in that folder so you can restore one.
  • Backups are stored by Google under your Google account and protected by your account's security. FleetBook does not add its own encryption to them. Google's handling of your Drive is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

How FleetBook uses Google user data

FleetBook uses your Google account information and Drive access only to sign you in and to back up and restore your records. It does not use them for advertising, does not sell or share them, and no one at Saadi Studios can read them. FleetBook's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we don't do

  • No ads, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No servers of our own. We do not receive, store, sell or share your records or account details.
  • FleetBook's data is left out of Android's own device backup and phone-to-phone transfer. It is backed up only to your Google Drive, as described above.

Sharing you choose

When you tap Share on a party's statement or on a profit & loss report, FleetBook writes a text message and opens Android's share menu. The text goes only to the app and the people you choose (for example WhatsApp). Tapping a party's call button opens your phone's dialer with their number. FleetBook does not make calls itself.

Permissions

FleetBook asks Android only for internet access, which it uses for Google sign-in and Drive backups.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Records stay on your phone until you delete them, sign out or uninstall FleetBook.
  • Signing out first backs up your records, then removes them from the phone.
  • Uninstalling FleetBook, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes everything on the phone.
  • To delete your backups: open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings > Manage apps > FleetBook > Options, and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • To remove FleetBook's access to your Google account: go to Third-party apps & services in your Google Account.

Security

Records on your phone are kept in FleetBook's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and are protected by your phone's screen lock and encryption. Everything FleetBook sends to Google uses encrypted connections. Anyone who can unlock your phone and open FleetBook can see your records, so keep your phone locked.

Children

FleetBook is a business tool for vehicle owners and is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
