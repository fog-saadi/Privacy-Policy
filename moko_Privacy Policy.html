Moko Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

In short: Moko reads which apps you use and for how long, only to decide how Moko the pet feels. That information is processed and stored on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else. Moko shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads.

This policy explains how the Moko app ("Moko", "we") by Saadi Studios handles information.

Information Moko reads on your phone

With your permission (Android's "Usage access" setting), Moko reads your phone's app usage history: which apps were open, when and for how long, and when the screen was turned on, off or unlocked. Moko uses this to work out things like your scrolling time, phone use after bedtime and focus sessions, and to set Moko's mood, streak and coins.

  • This information is processed entirely on your phone.
  • It is never uploaded, shared or sold. We do not have servers that receive it.
  • Moko keeps a daily summary (for example "42 minutes of scrolling") on your phone so streaks work over time.
  • You can turn off Usage access at any time in your phone's settings. Moko then stops reading usage.

Information Moko stores

Your settings, daily summaries, coins, streak freezes, shop items and focus sessions are stored only in Moko's private storage on your phone. Moko has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or messages.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Moko tells you when a focus adventure ends and sends a short morning report. You can turn these off in Moko's settings or in your phone's settings.

Advertising

Moko is free and shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and settings, and how you interact with ads. This is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Moko asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalized. You can change your choice any time from Moko's settings ("Privacy choices").
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Moko's app usage information is never shared with Google or any advertiser.

Children

Moko is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Because everything Moko stores is on your phone, you can delete all of it by uninstalling Moko or by clearing its storage in your phone's app settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
