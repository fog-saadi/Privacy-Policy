Delete your FleetBook data

FleetBook by Saadi Studios · Last updated: 5 October 2026

In short: FleetBook has no servers, so there is no account stored with us. Your data is in two places: on your phone, and in backups in your own Google Drive. You can delete both yourself, at any time, and deletion takes effect immediately.

Delete everything from inside the app

  1. Open FleetBook and tap the Settings icon on the Home screen.
  2. Under Google account, tap Delete all my data.
  3. Confirm. FleetBook deletes every backup from your Google Drive and all records on your phone, then signs you out.

Delete without the app

  1. Backups: open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings > Manage apps > FleetBook > Options, and choose Delete hidden app data.
  2. Access to your Google account: go to Third-party apps & services, choose FleetBook and remove its access.
  3. On the phone: uninstall FleetBook, or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: all vehicles, trips, expenses, payments, parties, notes, reminders and settings, on the phone and in every Google Drive backup.
  • Kept by us: nothing. We never receive your records, so we hold no copy.
  • Kept by Google: if you bought FleetBook Pro, Google Play keeps the purchase record under its own policies. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.
