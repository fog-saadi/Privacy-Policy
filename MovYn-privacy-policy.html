MovYn Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short: MovYn keeps your business records (vehicles, trips, expenses, parties, money accounts and payments) on your phone. You sign in with your Google account, and MovYn backs up your records to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. We have no servers and never receive your records. MovYn has no ads and no analytics.

This policy explains how the MovYn app ("MovYn", "we") by S&S Tech Studio handles information.

Information you enter

MovYn stores what you type into it:

  • Vehicles: name, registration number, make and model, notes, and oil change reminder settings.
  • Parties (customers, suppliers, drivers): name, phone number, type, opening balance and notes.
  • Trips: date, route, customer, driver, freight, amounts received and where they went, odometer readings, status and notes.
  • Expenses and payments: date, category, amount, amount paid, litres of fuel, meter reading, party, the account or driver the money came from or went to, the trip it was for, and notes.
  • Money accounts you add (cash in hand, bank accounts, mobile wallets): the name you give each one, its type, starting balance and notes, and transfers between them. MovYn never connects to your bank or wallet; it only keeps the figures you type in.
  • Settings: how often to back up, the Wi-Fi-only choice and your currency symbol.

These records are kept in MovYn's private storage on your phone. MovYn does not read your phone's contacts, location, photos or files. The only names and phone numbers it holds are the ones you type in.

Google sign-in

To use MovYn you sign in with your Google account. Google gives MovYn your name, email address and profile picture. MovYn keeps only your name and email address, on your phone. It uses them to show which account is signed in and to find that account's backups. MovYn never sees your Google password.

Backups to your Google Drive

  • With your permission, MovYn gets one limited kind of access to Google Drive, which Google describes as "See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive" (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata). It lets MovYn use a hidden folder that only MovYn can open. MovYn cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.
  • A backup is a compressed copy of all the records listed above. It goes directly from your phone to your Google Drive over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We never receive it.
  • Backups run daily or weekly, as you choose (you can also turn them off), and whenever you tap Back up now. The newest 10 backups are kept, and older ones are deleted automatically.
  • When you sign in on a new phone, MovYn lists the backups in that folder so you can restore one.
  • Backups are stored by Google under your Google account and protected by your account's security. MovYn does not add its own encryption to them. Google's handling of your Drive is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

How MovYn uses Google user data

MovYn uses your Google account information and Drive access only to sign you in and to back up and restore your records. It does not use them for advertising, does not sell or share them, and no one at S&S Tech Studio can read them. MovYn's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we don't do

  • No ads, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No servers of our own. We do not receive, store, sell or share your records or account details.
  • MovYn's data is left out of Android's own device backup and phone-to-phone transfer. It is backed up only to your Google Drive, as described above.

Sharing you choose

When you tap Share on a party's statement or on a profit & loss report, MovYn writes a text message and opens Android's share menu. The text goes only to the app and the people you choose (for example WhatsApp). Tapping a party's call button opens your phone's dialer with their number. MovYn does not make calls itself.

Oil change reminders

If you set an oil change reminder on a vehicle, MovYn compares the meter readings you enter with the reminder and shows a notification when an oil change is due. This happens entirely on your phone.

MovYn Pro subscription

MovYn Pro is an optional subscription sold through Google Play. Google Play handles the purchase and your payment details; MovYn never sees them. The app only asks Google Play whether your account has an active subscription, and keeps that answer on your phone so it works offline. Google's handling of purchases is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

MovYn asks Android for:

  • Internet access, for Google sign-in, Drive backups and checking your subscription.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later asks you), only to show oil change reminders. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • To delete everything at once, open Settings in MovYn and tap Delete all my data. It deletes every backup in your Google Drive and all records on your phone, then signs you out. Step-by-step help, including how to do this without the app, is on the Delete your MovYn data page.
  • Records stay on your phone until you delete them, sign out or uninstall MovYn.
  • Signing out first backs up your records, then removes them from the phone.
  • Uninstalling MovYn, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes everything on the phone.
  • To delete your backups: open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings > Manage apps > MovYn > Options, and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • To remove MovYn's access to your Google account: go to Third-party apps & services in your Google Account.

Security

Records on your phone are kept in MovYn's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and are protected by your phone's screen lock and encryption. Everything MovYn sends to Google uses encrypted connections. Anyone who can unlock your phone and open MovYn can see your records, so keep your phone locked.

Children

MovYn is a business tool for vehicle owners and is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
