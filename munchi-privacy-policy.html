Munchi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

In short: Munchi is a block puzzle game with a virtual pet. Your progress (scores, coins, your pet's name and looks) is stored only on your phone. We have no accounts and no servers. Munchi shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads.

This policy explains how the Munchi app ("Munchi", "we") by Saadi Studios handles information.

Information stored on your phone

  • Game progress: scores, journey level, coins, berries, streak days and the items you have unlocked.
  • Your pet's name, look and needs (hunger and happiness).
  • Your settings: sound, vibration and reminders.

This information never leaves your phone. We do not receive, share or sell it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Munchi is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, full-screen ads between games, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for rewards. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and app interactions with ads. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Munchi asks for your consent using Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on most Android phones).
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Munchi may send at most one reminder a day (for example when your pet is hungry or a daily gift is waiting). Reminders are created on your phone; no data is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings inside the game or in your phone's settings.

Children

Munchi is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
