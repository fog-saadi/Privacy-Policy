Nomlet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short: Nomlet is a block puzzle game with a virtual pet. Your progress (scores, coins, your pet's name and looks) is stored only on your phone. We have no accounts and no servers. Nomlet shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads, and uses Google Firebase to measure how the game is played and to adjust game settings. If you use Google Play Games, the trophies you earn are also sent to it as achievements.

This policy explains how the Nomlet app ("Nomlet", "we") by Saadi Studios handles information. Nomlet was called Munchi until October 2026; only the name changed.

Information stored on your phone

  • Game progress: scores, journey level, coins, berries, streak days and the items you have unlocked.
  • Your pet's name, look and needs (hunger and happiness).
  • Your settings: sound, vibration and reminders.
  • The trophies you have earned and collected.

This information stays on your phone, except trophies sent to Google Play Games as described below. We do not receive, share or sell it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Classic mode plays under Nomlet's sky of the day, which is worked out on your phone and needs no network. (Version 0.4.0 could instead play in a city's real weather, which sent the city's location to a weather service. That option is gone; updating removes any city saved by it.)

Achievements (Google Play Games)

Nomlet's trophies are kept on your phone. If you use Google Play Games, Nomlet also sends the trophies you earn to it as achievements, so they show in your Play Games profile. Play Games uses the Google profile already on your phone; Nomlet has no accounts of its own. If you don't have a Play Games profile, Nomlet doesn't ask you to make one unless you tap the Google Play Games button on the Trophies screen. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy; to support the service, the Play Games software also collects diagnostics about its own working. You can see or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account.

Game statistics and settings (Google Firebase)

To make Nomlet better, it uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how the game is played: for example which levels players start and finish, scores, trophies won, coins earned and spent, and which optional videos are watched. Firebase also collects some device information (such as an app instance ID, device model and approximate location from your IP address) and diagnostics. This information is not linked to your name, and we do not sell it. Analytics only starts after the privacy choices described below have been checked; in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland it follows the choices you make in the consent form, and you can change them in Settings > Privacy choices.

Nomlet also uses Firebase Remote Config to adjust game settings, such as rewards, difficulty and how often ads appear, without an app update. To do this it fetches settings at most once a day, identified by a random Firebase installation ID. Google handles this information under Firebase's privacy and security terms.

Updates (Google Play)

When a newer version of Nomlet is on Google Play, Nomlet asks Google Play about it and may offer to update. This uses Google Play's own update service; no data is sent to us.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Nomlet is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, full-screen ads between games, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for rewards. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and app interactions with ads. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Nomlet asks for your consent using Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on most Android phones).
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Nomlet may send at most one reminder a day (for example when your pet is hungry, a daily gift is waiting, or wild weather is falling on the board). Reminders are created on your phone; no data is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings inside the game or in your phone's settings.

Children

Nomlet is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
